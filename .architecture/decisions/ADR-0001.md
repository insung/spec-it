# Context

AI-assisted implementation became inconsistent across architecture, testing, comments, infrastructure, and operational decisions. A private document hub could preserve personal research but could not serve as a shareable project policy source.

# Options

| Option | Cost | Sharing | Machine use | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Keep narrative notes only | low initially | limited | weak | interpretation drift |
| Copy all common rules into each project | medium | possible | medium | update divergence |
| Public versioned SSOT plus small project projection | medium | strong | strong | schema and lifecycle maintenance |

# Decision

Use a separate public-capable `spec-it` repository. Projects store a short `AGENTS.md`, approved manifest, deterministic-lock target, ADRs, and active change spec. Common rule text remains in this repository; private personal and company overlays remain separate.

# Consequences

- The source is shareable without company information.
- Projects pin exact SemVer and do not auto-upgrade.
- Phase 0 can guide humans and agents but cannot claim executable enforcement.
- Phase 1 needs a minimal resolver and validator before CI enforcement.
